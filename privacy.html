Website analytics & privacy

Updated October 19, 2026 · 中文

Your choice

We do not automatically display an analytics popup. Analytics stays off unless you have opted in. To review your preference, open “Analytics choices” in the Projects page footer.

Optional Microsoft Clarity analytics starts only after you select “Allow analytics.” You can decline, or change your choice at any time using “Analytics choices” in the website footer. Declining does not prevent you from using the site or contacting Frank. We remember your choice in your browser for up to 180 days. Global Privacy Control and Do Not Track signals keep this analytics off.

What the analytics captures

With your permission, Microsoft Clarity collects website usage data such as pages visited, referrers, browser and device information, approximate location, time spent, clicks, scrolling, and mouse movement. It uses cookies and similar technologies for session analytics, heatmaps and session replays. These replays reconstruct interactions within this website; they do not record your screen outside the website.

We use these reports to understand which sections help visitors, where navigation is confusing, and how to improve the site. Advertising consent is set to denied in this integration. Analytics does not give Frank your name or email simply because you visited.

Forms and masking

Clarity masks input contents, and we additionally mask our signup and deal dialogs. Our custom analytics events use fixed action labels rather than typed values, contact details, clipboard contents or personal identifiers. Please do not put personal or confidential information into website links or campaign tracking parameters.

Information you intentionally submit through a signup form or send to Frank is handled separately from optional behavior analytics. The form’s own notice describes its purpose. Our hosting and form providers may process technical information needed to deliver their services, independently of this analytics choice.

Microsoft and your information

Usage data from opted-in visits is sent to Microsoft for Clarity processing. Read the Microsoft Privacy Statement and Clarity privacy information for details about Microsoft’s collection and use, storage, retention and privacy rights. Turning analytics off stops further recording on this page; it does not erase data already processed.

Visitor email matching from the portfolio link

Clicking the left-side “View Frank’s Apartments” portfolio link loads Google Tag Manager (container GTM-K9VHHGFL) and Retention.com’s collection script on the Projects page for that navigation. There is no email-matching permission popup. Direct visits, other links, reloads, Global Privacy Control and Do Not Track do not activate this collection.

Retention.com receives browser and visit information and may match an eligible visitor to an email address using its identity network for follow-up from Frank. A visit does not guarantee an email match. This collection is separate from optional Microsoft Clarity analytics. See Retention.com’s privacy policy and Google’s privacy policy for provider processing and privacy choices.

网站分析与隐私

更新日期：2026 年 10 月 19 日

您的选择

网站不会自动弹出分析提示。除非您已明确同意，否则分析保持关闭。您可以通过项目页面页脚的“分析设置”查看或修改选择。

只有在您选择“允许分析”后，Microsoft Clarity 网站分析才会启动。您可以拒绝，或随时通过网站页脚的“分析设置”修改选择。拒绝不影响浏览或联系 Frank。选择会在浏览器中保留最多 180 天。浏览器的 Global Privacy Control 或 Do Not Track 信号会关闭此分析。

收集的信息与用途

经您同意后，Clarity 会通过 Cookie 等技术记录访问页面、来源、浏览器与设备信息、大致位置、停留时间、点击、滚动和鼠标移动，用于热力图和会话回放。回放仅重现本网站内的操作，不会录制网站之外的屏幕。我们使用这些信息改善内容与导航。本集成将广告同意状态设为拒绝；仅仅访问网站不会向 Frank 提供您的姓名或邮箱。

表单与数据处理

Clarity 会遮蔽输入内容，我们还额外遮蔽了注册和房源提交弹窗。自定义事件仅使用固定的操作名称，不读取填写内容、联系方式、剪贴板内容或个人标识。请勿在网址或推广参数中放入个人或保密信息。

您主动通过表单或邮件提交的信息，与可选的行为分析分开处理，请参阅表单自身的说明。网站托管与表单服务商可能独立处理提供服务所需的技术信息。同意分析后的使用数据将发送给 Microsoft，详情请参阅 Microsoft 隐私声明及 Clarity 隐私说明。关闭分析会停止此页面的后续记录，但不会删除已经处理的数据。

如有疑问，请联系 frank.fan@moohousing.com。
